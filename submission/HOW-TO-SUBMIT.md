# Submitting this

## What "make a copy of the Template" means

The PDF you were sent is an export of a Google Doc template that Flipkart shared
read-only. You are meant to open that Google Doc link and use **File > Make a copy**,
which drops an editable copy into your own Drive. Then you type into your copy.

The reason they spell it out: people who try to edit the shared original get an
"ask for edit access" prompt, and the brief says those requests will be ignored.
So never click Request access. Just make your own copy.

The template has four pages:
  p1  cover: problem statement + your details   <- fill in the yellow placeholders
  p2  leading questions + submission rules      <- leave as is
  p3  blank                                     <- your answer
  p4  blank                                     <- your answer

Two ways to finish, pick one:

A. The PDF is ready to upload as it is. If you want to change any wording,
   edit the .docx and export a fresh PDF from Word (File > Save as > PDF),
   keeping A4 and portrait.

B. Make a copy of their Google Doc, then paste the text from body-text.txt
   into pages 3 and 4. Set the body font to 10.5pt. This keeps their exact
   cover page and logo.

Either way the final upload must be a PDF.
