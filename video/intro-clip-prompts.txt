VERIGATE DEMO - INTRO CLIPS
Two 8-second clips to sit in front of the screen recording.
Paste each block into Gemini separately. Upload your reference photo first.

================================================================
BEFORE YOU GENERATE
================================================================

1. Upload your photo in the same message as the prompt, and generate CLIP 1
   and CLIP 2 in separate messages (one clip per generation).

2. Keep your photo the same for both so the face matches. The hoodie is
   described identically in both prompts on purpose.

3. Set output to 16:9 so it matches your screen recording.

4. 8 seconds is the native clip length. If your version offers 10, both
   prompts stretch fine; just slow the camera moves.

5. The white flash at the end of clip 1 and the start of clip 2 is there to
   hide any mismatch between the two generations. Keep it.

================================================================
CLIP 1 - LEAVING 2026
================================================================

8-second live-action cinematic shot, 16:9, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field.

SUBJECT: The young Indian man from the attached reference photo. Same face,
same hair. Mid-twenties, wearing a charcoal grey hoodie over a plain t-shirt.

SETTING: A small cluttered student bedroom at night. One warm desk lamp, an
open laptop, printed papers and a coffee mug. On the wall behind him, a paper
calendar for OCTOBER 2026 with the 24th circled in red marker.

ACTION, in this order:
  0-2s   Close on his hands holding a scuffed handheld device with a glowing
         amber segment display reading 24 OCT 2026. He looks up from it.
  2-4s   He presses a large button on the side. A ring of blue-white light
         tears open in the middle of the room, about two metres tall, its edge
         rippling like liquid glass. Loose papers lift off the desk in the draft.
  4-7s   The amber digits spin rapidly upward and settle hard on 04 MAR 2040.
         The camera pushes in past his shoulder toward the ring.
  7-8s   He steps into it. The light flares to pure white and fills the frame.

CAMERA: Handheld and close to start, then a smooth dolly push-in toward the
ring. Ends on a full white frame.

LIGHT: Warm tungsten lamp, then cold blue portal light taking over his face.

ON-SCREEN TEXT: Small clean white sans-serif, lower left, visible only for the
first two seconds: "Bengaluru, 24 October 2026". No other text anywhere.

AUDIO: Quiet night room tone. A mechanical button click. A low electrical hum
rising in pitch. Papers fluttering. A deep bass whoosh as the ring opens, and
one low synth note swelling from 4s.
NO DIALOGUE. NO VOICEOVER. NO SPOKEN WORDS. No narration of any kind.

AVOID: cartoon or animated look, neon sci-fi styling, other people in the room,
brand logos, any text other than the date, lens flare across his face, speech.

================================================================
CLIP 2 - ARRIVING IN 2040
================================================================

8-second live-action cinematic shot, 16:9, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field.
This continues directly from the previous shot.

OPENING: The frame starts pure white and the white falls away within the first
half second to reveal the new room.

SUBJECT: The same young Indian man from the attached reference photo. Same
face, same hair, same charcoal grey hoodie.

SETTING: A bright calm apartment, mid-morning daylight through a large window.
Pale wood, clean simple furniture, a couple of plants. Newer and tidier than
the previous room, but ordinary. Not futuristic.

SECOND PERSON: A young Indian woman in her late twenties, sitting at a desk
with her back three-quarters to camera. Dark hair tied back, olive green
sweater. She is working calmly on a thin modern laptop.

ACTION, in this order:
  0-1s   The white resolves. He stumbles one step forward into the room,
         steadies himself and looks around, surprised.
  1-4s   He notices her, walks over quietly and stops just behind her left
         shoulder, leaning in to see her screen. She does not react to him.
         He is watching, not interrupting.
  4-7s   Over-the-shoulder view of the laptop. The screen shows a calm
         cream-white dashboard: a dark serif heading at the top and a neat
         vertical list of rows below it, one row tinted pale yellow, small
         coloured labels down the right-hand edge. Keep it soft and slightly
         out of focus. She clicks one row. He leans in, interested.
  7-8s   The camera pushes smoothly past him into the laptop screen until the
         screen fills the frame and goes gently soft.

CAMERA: Wide to establish the room, then a slow steady dolly in, finishing on
a tight push into the screen.

LIGHT: Soft natural daylight, gentle contrast, slight warm cast.

ON-SCREEN TEXT: Small clean white sans-serif, lower left, visible only between
1 and 3 seconds: "4 March 2040". No other text anywhere.

AUDIO: An airy whoosh settling into quiet room tone. Faint birds outside. A
soft keyboard tap and one quiet interface click around 5s. A calm sustained
synth pad underneath that fades out at the end.
NO DIALOGUE. NO VOICEOVER. NO SPOKEN WORDS.

AVOID: neon, holograms, flying cars, robots, cartoon look, dense unreadable
gibberish text on the laptop, brand logos, any speech.

================================================================
IF THE RESULT ISN'T RIGHT
================================================================

Dates come out garbled
  Likely. Video models are poor at rendering text. Easiest fix: delete the
  ON-SCREEN TEXT line from the prompt, regenerate, and add the two date
  captions yourself in your editor. That also lets you match the font to
  your demo.

It adds a voice anyway
  Regenerate. If it persists, put "silent film, ambient sound only" at the
  very start of the prompt.

His face doesn't match
  Re-upload the photo in the same message as the prompt, and use a clear,
  front-facing, well-lit photo of just you.

The two clips look like different rooms or different people
  Expected to a degree. The white flash covers most of it. You can also trim
  harder into the flash on both sides when you cut them together.

The portal looks silly
  Swap the ring for something plainer: replace the portal sentences with
  "the air in front of him distorts and folds like heat haze over a road,
  bending the room behind it."

================================================================
PUTTING IT TOGETHER
================================================================

Order:  clip 1  ->  clip 2  ->  your screen recording

Cut from clip 1 to clip 2 at the brightest frame of the white flash.

Cut from clip 2 into the recording at the moment the laptop screen fills the
frame. Your recording opens on the Overview page, which is what Ananya is
looking at, so the push into her screen becomes the push into the real app.

A half-second cross-dissolve on that second cut hides the resolution change.

Your narration starts on the first frame of the screen recording, on
"Picture this. It's 2040." The two intro clips stay silent apart from their
own effects, so nothing competes with your voice.
